# PASSPORT playtest

Play ten rounds with 4-10 people. Use an existing voice call or sit together. Keep private roles off shared screens. Rotate who hosts, try both early voting and the full discussion timer, and let the Tourist’s final guess play out.

For each round, briefly note the country, player count, whether the Tourist was caught, the final guess if any, the winner, and one memorable moment. No names or contact details are needed. Note any confusing control, awkward pause, or layout problem as it happens.

After 10 rounds, ask:

1. Were people actually laughing/arguing?
2. Did the Tourist have a realistic chance?
3. Were the same questions repeated too often?
4. Were any countries too obscure?
5. Were any countries too easy?
6. Did voting feel too early or too late?
7. Was the final guess too powerful?
8. Did players immediately want another round?
9. Did anyone misunderstand the rules?
10. What moment produced the biggest reaction?

Pick the single change most likely to improve the next ten rounds. Keep rule changes separate from visual or reliability fixes so their effects are easier to understand.
